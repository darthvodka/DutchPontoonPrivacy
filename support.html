Contact

For help with Dutch Pontoon (bugs, multiplayer issues, privacy questions, or App Store feedback), email oliver.penney16@icloud.com.

Please include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of what went wrong. Screenshots help.

Common topics

  • Ads / tracking: You can decline App Tracking Transparency and still play. In some regions, use Privacy choices in the app to change ad preferences. System path: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Multiplayer: Everyone needs a network connection and the same room code. Rooms expire after inactivity. Display names are local nicknames — don’t put personal contact details in them.
  • Crash dump: If a one-player game fails uncleanly, the app may offer a text dump you can copy into an email.

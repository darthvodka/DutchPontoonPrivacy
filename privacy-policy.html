Dutch Pontoon — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Who wrote this?

Dutch Pontoon is a single-player card game app by Oliver Penney. The game itself runs on your device and does not require an account.

What the app does

Gameplay (dealing, scoring, CPU opponents) stays on your device. We do not operate a gameplay server and we do not sell your personal information.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The home screen may show a banner advertisement from Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect device and advertising information such as:

  • Advertising identifier (IDFA), if you allow tracking
  • IP address (often used to estimate coarse location)
  • Device and app information
  • Ad interaction and diagnostic data

This data may be used to show ads, measure ads, combat fraud, and improve Google’s advertising services. Some of this activity may constitute “tracking” under Apple’s App Tracking Transparency rules when you grant permission.

Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Your choices

  • App Tracking Transparency: iOS may ask whether Dutch Pontoon may track you across other companies’ apps and websites for personalized ads. You can change this later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Privacy & messaging (UMP): In regions that require it (for example the EEA/UK), you may see a consent form before ads load. Where required, use Privacy choices in the app to change your ad preferences.
  • You can still use the game if you decline personalised advertising; ads may be less relevant or limited.

Children

Dutch Pontoon is a card game of chance and skill with rules that are not aimed at young children. It is not directed at children under 13, and it is not intended for the App Store Kids Category.

Data retention & deletion

We do not maintain a user account database for this app. Advertising data is handled by Google according to their policies. Uninstalling the app removes local app data from your device.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the support contact on the Dutch Pontoon App Store product page, or email oliver.penney16@icloud.com.

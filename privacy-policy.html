Dutch Pontoon — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Who we are

Dutch Pontoon is a card game app by Oliver Penney. You can play offline against computer opponents, or online with friends via multiplayer rooms. We do not sell your personal information.

Summary

  • Solo play and achievements stay on your device.
  • Multiplayer uses Google Firebase (anonymous sign-in and Realtime Database) so rooms can sync.
  • The home screen may show a Google AdMob banner. AdMob and its partners may collect advertising and device data; some of that can be “tracking” under Apple’s rules if you allow it.
  • We do not require an email/password account, and we do not run our own analytics product in the app.

Data on your device

The app may store locally on your device (for example in app preferences):

  • Game settings and preferences
  • Achievement progress
  • Optional crash / debug dumps if a one-player session fails, so you can share them if you choose

This local data is not uploaded by Dutch Pontoon to our own servers. Uninstalling the app removes it from the device.

Multiplayer (Google Firebase)

Online multiplayer uses Google Firebase:

  • Anonymous Authentication — a random user ID so you can join rooms without creating a username/password account
  • Realtime Database — short-lived room data so players stay in sync

Room data may include: a room code, your chosen display name, seat assignments, ready/connected status, game state (cards, scores, turns), series win tallies, and in-game chat or table messages you or the table send.

Display names are whatever you type; avoid putting personal contact details in names or chat. Multiplayer rooms are intended for play among people who share a room code. Rooms age out and may be removed after inactivity (typically within about a day).

Firebase is operated by Google. See Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The home screen may show a banner ad from Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect or process information such as:

  • Advertising identifier (IDFA), if you allow tracking
  • IP address (often used to estimate coarse location)
  • Device and app information
  • Ad interaction and diagnostic / performance data

This data may be used to show ads, measure ads, combat fraud, and improve Google’s advertising services. Some of this activity may constitute “tracking” under Apple’s App Tracking Transparency rules when you grant permission.

Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Google’s AdMob data disclosure guidance for developers: https://developers.google.com/admob/ios/privacy/data-disclosure

Your choices

  • App Tracking Transparency: iOS may ask whether Dutch Pontoon may track you across other companies’ apps and websites for personalized ads. You can change this later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Privacy & messaging (UMP): In regions that require it (for example the EEA/UK), you may see a consent form before ads load. Where required, use Privacy choices in the app to change your ad preferences.
  • You can still use the game if you decline personalized advertising; ads may be less relevant or limited.
  • You can avoid multiplayer entirely; solo play does not need Firebase rooms.

Children

Dutch Pontoon is a card game of chance and skill. It is not directed at children under 13, and it is not intended for the App Store Kids Category. Parents should supervise younger players.

Retention & deletion

We do not maintain a separate customer account database for Dutch Pontoon. Advertising data is handled by Google under its policies. Multiplayer room data lives in Firebase Realtime Database and is temporary for gameplay; rooms are removed when abandoned or after they age out. Uninstalling the app removes local app data from your device. Leaving a multiplayer room stops your active participation; residual room data may remain until the room is cleaned up.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app’s features or third-party services change. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
